Meet Our Team

Shigeru Miyamoto

designers

Widely regarded as one of the most accomplished and influential designers in the history of video games, he is often called the "father of modern video gaming". His work is praised for pioneering new genres (like the platformer) and game design concepts such as open-world, non-linear gameplay, and in-game saving using battery backup. Developers praise his focus on user experience and creating "fun" gameplay, but some critics note that his insistence on radical innovation for every sequel can sometimes be a detriment to certain franchises.

John Carmack

programmer

Carmack is lauded for his technical expertise, perseverance, and high-quality code. He is respected for openly sharing his engine's source code, which allowed a generation of other developers to get their start modifying his games and contributed significantly to the growth of the industry. His work in real-time 3D graphics made complex environments possible on home computers and has been described as a "masterpiece" of programming. In more recent years, he has been a major figure in the development of modern Virtual Reality (VR) technology at Oculus/Meta.

Hideo Kojima

Kojima's games are highly polarizing; he is considered by many to be a visionary auteur who pushes the boundaries of interactive storytelling. His work is praised for deep, emotional narratives and innovative, ahead-of-their-time game mechanics. On the other hand, critics sometimes describe his work as "self-indulgent nonsense" with overly long cutscenes and bizarre narrative choices that can detract from the gameplay. Regardless of opinion, his unique, film-inspired style consistently generates strong reactions and discussion within the gaming community.
